About Christian

Buying or selling a home is one of the biggest financial decisions you'll make, and you deserve to understand every step of it. My approach is simple: explain the process clearly, show you what the numbers are really saying, and make sure you never feel rushed or left in the dark.

With a background in real estate investing, I look at every property through two lenses: how it fits your life today, and how it's likely to perform over time. That perspective helps my clients across St. Catharines and the Niagara region spot real value, avoid costly surprises and negotiate with confidence.

You'll always get honest advice and realistic expectations from me, even when it isn't what you want to hear. I'm in this for long-term relationships, so I'm still here after closing day whenever you have a question about the market or your next move.
