About Christian

Your Niagara real estate agent: honest advice, an investor's eye and service that continues after closing day.

Hi, I'm Christian

I'm a REALTOR® with RE/MAX Niagara Realty Ltd., based in St. Catharines and serving buyers, sellers, landlords and investors across the Niagara region and Hamilton.

Buying or selling a home is one of the biggest financial decisions you'll make, and you deserve to understand every step of it. I take the time to explain the process clearly, walk you through what the numbers are really saying and make sure you never feel rushed or left in the dark.

An investor's perspective

My background in real estate investing shapes how I look at every property: how it fits your life today, and how it's likely to perform over time. Whether you're buying your first home, upgrading for a growing family or adding a rental to your portfolio, that perspective helps you spot real value, avoid costly surprises and negotiate with confidence.

What you can expect from me

  • Clear communication. Quick replies and straight answers, by phone, text or email.
  • Honest advice. Realistic expectations on price, timing and condition, even when it isn't what you want to hear.
  • Local knowledge. Street-level insight into St. Catharines, Niagara Falls, Welland, Thorold, Pelham and surrounding communities.
  • Long-term relationships. I'm still here after closing day whenever you have a question about the market or your next move.

Backed by RE/MAX

As part of RE/MAX Niagara Realty Ltd., I combine personal, one-on-one service with the marketing reach and resources of one of the most recognized names in real estate.
