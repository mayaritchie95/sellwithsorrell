Frequently asked questions

How much do I need for a down payment in Ontario?

The minimum down payment depends on the purchase price, and anything under 20% requires mortgage default insurance. Rules change from time to time, so I recommend confirming current requirements with a mortgage professional. I'm happy to connect you with trusted local lenders and brokers.

Does it cost anything to work with a buyer's agent?

In most Niagara purchases the buyer agent's commission is paid out of the sale proceeds by the seller. We'll review exactly how compensation works in our buyer representation agreement before you commit to anything.

How long does it take to buy a home?

Many buyers find the right home within a few weeks to a few months. Once an offer is accepted, closings are typically set 30 to 90 days out, depending on what works for you and the seller.

Should I sell my current home first or buy first?

It depends on your finances and the market. Buying first gives you certainty on your next home; selling first gives you certainty on your budget. We'll weigh both options, including bridge financing, based on your situation.
