Real Estate Investing

Work with an agent who looks at every property like an investor.

Investing in Niagara Real Estate

Real estate investing is personal for me, and it's a big part of how I approach every deal. I look past the listing photos to the numbers: rent potential, carrying costs, cash flow and long-term appreciation.

How I Help Investors

  • Deal analysis: realistic rent estimates, expenses and cash-flow projections before you make an offer
  • Finding opportunities: properties with suite potential, value-add upgrades or strong rental demand
  • Area insight: where demand is coming from, including students, commuters and healthcare and tourism workers
  • Leasing support: marketing your unit and helping you find qualified tenants
  • Portfolio strategy: when to hold, refinance or sell to grow your portfolio

Popular Niagara Investment Areas

St. Catharines and Thorold near Brock University, Welland near Niagara College, and Niagara Falls with its tourism-driven economy each offer different strategies and price points.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Niagara a Good Place to Invest in Real Estate?

Niagara offers lower entry prices than the GTA, a diverse rental base including students, healthcare and tourism workers, and ongoing infrastructure investment. Every property is different, so we'll analyze each deal on its own numbers.

What Types of Investment Properties Do You Help With?

Single-family rentals, duplexes and triplexes, homes with secondary suites, student rentals near Brock University and Niagara College, and small multi-unit buildings.

Can You Help Me Find Tenants?

Yes. I can help market your rental and screen prospective tenants, as I've done for landlord clients.
